What Redactly AI does

Built for law offices, healthcare, finance, and anyone who handles sensitive documents.

🧠

Detect and redact by category

Toggle detection for SSNs, account numbers, card numbers, dates of birth, phone numbers, emails, addresses, driver's license numbers, and other IDs. Each category has its own switch so you control exactly what gets flagged.

🔒

On-device processing

Scanning, OCR, detection, and redaction all run on your device. Documents are not uploaded to our servers. The one exception: if you choose to fax a document (mobile only), it is sent through our fax provider, Telnyx.

🔥

Permanent, physical redaction

Black bars are burned into the pixel layer — the underlying text is gone. The exported PDF is a flattened image with nothing to scrape, copy, or undo.

✏️

Review and undo

Tap any black box to remove just that one redaction, with full Undo/Redo support. A pre-export safety check helps you catch anything you may have missed before saving.

📄

Import PDFs, photos, or scans

Open a PDF, import a photo, or use your device camera to scan a paper document. All processed on-device — exported as a permanently-redacted PDF.

🎯

Simple commands

Tell Redactly which names to protect and it finds every variation. Use commands like "Redact all SSNs" or "Redact all phone numbers" to flag matches across every page.

🗄️

Local scan history

Your document library is stored on your device. Past scans are accessible without an internet connection.

📠

Outbound fax (mobile only)

Fax a redacted document from the iPhone or Android app — send-only, no receiving. Enter the number and send. The document is transmitted through our fax provider, Telnyx.
