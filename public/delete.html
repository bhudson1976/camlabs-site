Data Deletion Request

Redactly processes your documents on your device and does not upload them to our servers. If you created an optional Redactly account, we store your account details, subscription status, fax credits, and fax logs (see our Privacy Policy).

Delete your Redactly account in the app

Open Redactly, go to Settings → Delete Account, and confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, subscription status, fax credits and history, and fax logs from our servers. Documents on your device are removed when you delete the app.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, or for any other CamLabs.ai product or website purchase, email support@camlabs.ai from the email address on your account and tell us what you'd like deleted.

Requests are typically processed within 30 days. We may keep limited transaction records where required for tax and accounting obligations.
