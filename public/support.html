🖊️ Redactly AI — PDF Scanner

Smart AI auto-redaction with 100% on-device privacy — true destructive vector redaction and precision scanning with PDF/PNG exports.

📸 Camera & Document Scanner Permissions

Redactly AI needs camera access to scan your documents. If scanning isn't working, enable it in your device settings: go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > Redactly AI and toggle it on, then reopen the app.

🔒 Privacy & Offline Processing

Your documents never leave your device. All scanning, OCR text recognition, and redactions happen 100% locally, on-device — with zero cloud uploads. Redactly AI works fully offline, and we never see, store, or transmit your files. Because redaction is truly destructive at the vector level, removed content is gone from the exported file, not just hidden behind a black box.

🔁 Restoring Purchases / Subscriptions

Already purchased Pro or an active subscription? Open Redactly AI, go to the Upgrade / Settings screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you used to buy. If your unlock still doesn't appear, fully close and reopen the app — or contact us below and we'll get it sorted.

💎 CaratCam & CaratCam Plus

📲 App Not Opening?

Make sure you've installed the latest version of the APK. Go to Downloads, tap the file, and allow "Install Unknown Apps" if prompted.

🔐 Purchase Not Unlocking Features?

Close and reopen the app. If that doesn't work, try uninstalling and reinstalling it. Still having trouble? Reach out below.

📸 Camera Access Issues?

Check your phone settings: go to Settings > Apps > CaratCam > Permissions, and make sure Camera access is turned on.
