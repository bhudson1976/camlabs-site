Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 25, 2026  ·  Entity: CamLabs.ai  ·  Contact: support@camlabs.ai

🔒 The short version

  • Our apps process your data on your device. Redactly and Sentinel AI do not collect, store, transmit, or share your documents, images, code, or personal information.
  • No tracking. We use no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in our applications.
  • We never sell your data. Not ever, to anyone.
  • The one exception is buying a license on this website, which necessarily involves your email address and a payment processor — explained in full below.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy describes how CamLabs.ai ("we", "our", or "us") handles information across our applications and this website. It applies to:

  • Redactly — our on-device document redaction utility
  • Sentinel AI — our local-first desktop security auditing application
  • camlabs.ai — this website, including license purchases and support

2. Redactly

Redactly is a local, on-device document redaction utility. It does not collect, store, track, transmit, or share any personal data, documents, images, or personally identifiable information. All processing occurs entirely locally on your device.

Your documents and images never leave your device through Redactly. We have no servers receiving them, no accounts to create, and no ability to see what you redact. Redactly contains no third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

3. Sentinel AI

Sentinel AI performs its security analysis entirely on your own machine using an on-device AI model. Your source code is never uploaded, logged, or transmitted to us or any third party. The application contains no telemetry, analytics, or tracking.

For transparency, Sentinel AI touches the network in only two one-time situations: downloading its AI model on first launch, and validating your license key during activation. After activation, it operates offline.

4. This Website & License Purchases

We believe in being precise rather than sweeping, so here is exactly what happens if you purchase a license on camlabs.ai:

  • Email address. We collect the email address you provide at checkout for one purpose: to deliver your license key and provide support. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen, handled, or stored by us.
  • License records. We store a record of your license (key, email, purchase reference, activation status) so we can validate your license and re-send it if you need it.
  • Transactional email. We use an email delivery provider to send your license key and support replies.

We do not use advertising networks, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs on this website.

5. Service Providers

We rely on a small number of reputable providers strictly to operate the business — never to profile you:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Supabase — secure storage of license records
  • Resend — delivery of transactional email (license keys, support)
  • Firebase Hosting and Cloudflare R2 — website hosting and application downloads

6. Data Retention

Because our applications collect nothing, there is nothing to retain from them. For purchases, we retain license records for as long as your lifetime license remains valid, and as required for tax and accounting obligations. You may request deletion of your purchase record at any time, understanding that this may prevent us from re-issuing your license.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because the only personal information we hold is tied to a purchase, these requests are simple to fulfill — email support@camlabs.ai and we will respond promptly.

8. Children's Privacy

Our applications and services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We minimize risk by minimizing collection — the safest data is the data we never gather. The limited purchase information we do hold is stored with access controls and transmitted over encrypted connections.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes affecting how we handle personal information will be described clearly.

11. Your Consent

By using our applications or this website, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.
