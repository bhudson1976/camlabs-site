Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026  ·  Entity: CamLabs.ai  ·  Contact: support@camlabs.ai

🔒 The short version

  • Your documents are processed on your device. Redactly scans, reads, and redacts your documents locally. We never upload them to our servers.
  • One exception: sending a fax. If you choose to fax a document from Redactly (mobile only), that document is transmitted through our fax provider, Telnyx, to the number you enter.
  • Accounts are optional. You can use Redactly without signing in. If you do sign in, we store your account details and purchase status so your subscription and fax credits work across devices.
  • No tracking or ads. We use no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking, and no third-party analytics in our apps.
  • We never sell your data. Not ever, to anyone.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy describes how CamLabs.ai ("we", "our", or "us") handles information across our applications and this website. It applies to:

  • Redactly — our document scanning and redaction app for iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows
  • Sentinel AI — our local-first desktop security auditing application
  • camlabs.ai — this website, including license purchases and support

2. Redactly

What stays on your device

Scanning, text recognition (OCR), sensitive-information detection, redaction, the document Library, and the built-in AI assistant all run on your device. Your documents, images, extracted text, and redaction history are stored locally and are not uploaded to our servers. We cannot see what you scan or redact.

On Android and iOS, text recognition uses Google's ML Kit, which runs on-device. Google may receive limited technical and usage information about the ML Kit library itself (not your document content), as described in Google's ML Kit terms.

Optional account

Signing in is optional and is only needed for cross-device features, purchases tied to your account, and faxing. If you create an account (with Apple, Google, or email and password), we store:

  • Account details — your email address, a user ID, and the name your sign-in provider shares (if any).
  • Purchase status — whether your subscription is active and when it renews or expires.
  • Fax credits — your credit balance and a history of credit purchases and use.

Sending a fax (mobile only)

When you choose to send a fax, the document you select is uploaded to Telnyx, our fax provider, which transmits it to the recipient. We keep a fax log (fax ID, the sending and receiving fax numbers, and delivery status) so we can report delivery and deduct credits. We do not keep a copy of the faxed document on our servers. Redactly does not receive faxes.

Subscriptions and purchases

In-app subscriptions and fax credits are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous or account-linked app user ID and your purchase history. We never see your payment card details. Desktop licenses bought on this website are covered in section 4.

AI model download

On supported devices, Redactly downloads its on-device AI model once. The download is authorized by our server and delivered from Hugging Face, which, like any web server, sees your IP address during the download. After that, the AI assistant runs on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, subscription status, fax credits and history, and fax logs from our servers. Documents stored on your device are removed when you delete the app. See our data deletion page for other options.

3. Sentinel AI

Sentinel AI performs its security analysis entirely on your own machine using an on-device AI model. Your source code is never uploaded, logged, or transmitted to us or any third party. The application contains no telemetry, analytics, or tracking.

For transparency, Sentinel AI touches the network in only two one-time situations: downloading its AI model on first launch, and validating your license key during activation. After activation, it operates offline.

4. This Website & License Purchases

We believe in being precise rather than sweeping, so here is exactly what happens if you purchase a license or start a trial on camlabs.ai:

  • Email address. We collect the email address you provide for one purpose: to deliver your license key or trial and provide support. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen, handled, or stored by us.
  • License records. We store a record of your license (key, email, purchase reference, activation status) so we can validate your license and re-send it if you need it.
  • Transactional email. We use an email delivery provider to send your license key and support replies.

We do not use advertising networks, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs on this website.

5. Service Providers

We rely on a small number of providers strictly to operate our apps and business — never to profile you or advertise to you:

  • Supabase — account sign-in and storage of account, purchase, fax-credit, and license records
  • Telnyx — fax transmission (only when you send a fax)
  • RevenueCat, Apple App Store, and Google Play — in-app subscriptions and purchases
  • Stripe — payment processing for website purchases
  • Resend — delivery of transactional email (license keys, support)
  • Hugging Face — hosting of the on-device AI model download
  • Firebase Hosting and Cloudflare R2 — website hosting and application downloads

6. Data Retention

Documents you process in our apps stay on your device, so we have none to retain. Account, purchase, and fax records are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your account, except where we must keep limited transaction records for tax and accounting obligations. License records for website purchases are kept for as long as your license remains valid and as required for tax and accounting. You may request deletion of a purchase record at any time, understanding that this may prevent us from re-issuing your license.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. You can delete your Redactly account directly in the app, or email support@camlabs.ai for any other request and we will respond promptly.

8. Children's Privacy

Our applications and services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We minimize risk by minimizing collection — the safest data is the data we never gather. The information we do hold is stored with access controls and transmitted over encrypted connections.
